## Post

roadtrackerindia.com is live.

Every numbered road in India on one map. 7,761 roads, 3,42,735 km.

Search any highway, or let GPS tell you which one you're on. See who built it and who's responsible for it. Read the news for that exact road — closures, landslides, potholes. Pin a pothole, and anyone can clear the pin once the road is actually fixed.

Every national highway page carries 1033 — NHAI's free 24×7 number for an ambulance, a crane, an accident, a blocked road. Most people have never heard of it. That one thing was worth building the site for.

No account, no login, no tracking. Free, and open source.
